Kecil-TTS 8M

by Suarify

Kecil-TTS-8M is a lightweight, Kokoro-style Malaysian Malay (Bahasa Melayu) text-to-speech model: 7.9 million parameters, under 10 MB, running entirely in your browser. No server, nothing you type leaves your device.

Effects lab (optional)

Shape the voice after it is made: tone, a delayed second layer (doubling / chorus), an echo tail and a small room. Everything runs in your browser, live while it plays. Pick a preset or move the sliders (double-click a slider to reset it).

3 · Runs anywhere: edge, IoT, kiosk, vending machine

  • Under 10 MB of weights: fits a Raspberry Pi, an Android kiosk, a mini-PC or a browser tab.
  • Runs on one CPU thread — no GPU, no internet, no per-request cost, no audio leaving the device.
  • Speaks in Bahasa Melayu Malaysia, including the English words Malaysians actually use.
  • Same ONNX file everywhere: browser (WebGPU/WASM), Python, Node, .NET, Java, or on-device SDKs.
